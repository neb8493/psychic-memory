# Script: "Snowball Earth"

Episode 2 of the deep-time series, same format as "Earth Gets a Ring": 30 blocks of 10 seconds, the Pastel Flat 2D look, Nia (field) and Theo (lab), narrator Cillian.

```

  1) Earth Froze Solid. Then It Did It Again.
  2) Snowball Earth: The Ice Age That Lasted 56 Million Years
  3) Earth Was Once a Snowball. A New Study Changes the Story
THUMBNAIL LINE: FROZEN TO THE EQUATOR
TARGET LENGTH: 5:00 (670 words of VO, 30 blocks x 10 s)
NICHE: explainer (deep-time Earth history)
HOOK SOURCE: Harvard SEAS / PNAS study, April 2026
```

Title 1 is the strongest, because the "again" is the new science. Avoid "Earth WAS a snowball" without qualifiers in the title. The full freeze is the leading idea, but block 27 covers the slushball alternative.

## Structure

- **Hook (1):** the whole planet froze, pole to equator.
- **Build, the evidence (2–6):** what it looked like, the name, glacial rocks at the equator, magnetic grains, dropstones.
- **Build, how it froze (7–12):** the ice-albedo feedback, the 30° runaway, the Canadian lava floods drawing down CO₂, the sealed planet, the thick ice.
- **Build, how it ended (13–20):** 56 million years, the escape puzzle, volcanic CO₂, the fast thaw, the hothouse, cap carbonates.
- **Turn (21):** the 2026 study, which says it froze and thawed repeatedly.
- **Build, what that explains (22–29):** the basalt reset loop, the rhythm, life and oxygen, refuges, the slushball debate, complex life afterwards.
- **Payoff (30):** every animal descends from the survivors; the ice left and the survivors stayed.

Through-line: **the ice sheet**. It spreads from the poles, seals the equator, thickens, gets punched through by volcanoes, melts, returns, thins over ponds and is gone by the last block, where Nia holds a cap-carbonate pebble in a hot tropical plaza.
